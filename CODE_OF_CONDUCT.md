# Code of conduct

Tessera is a small research project, and I'd like it to be a place where anyone
interested in networks-on-chip feels welcome, whether you're a student running
your first simulation or someone who has been building routers for years.

The short version:

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Disagree about ideas, not about people. Assume good intent.
- No harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes, in any channel connected to
  the project (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews).
- Give credit. If your work builds on someone else's paper or code, say so.

If someone makes you uncomfortable, or you see something that crosses the line,
contact the maintainer ([@amin-norollah](https://github.com/amin-norollah))
privately. Reports are handled in confidence. Anyone who keeps breaking these
rules may be asked to leave the project.

This is based on the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
